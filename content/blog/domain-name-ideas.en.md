## Why domain names matter

A good domain name is short, memorable, and hints at what you do. Here are a few rules we follow:

- **Keep it short** — Under 10 characters is ideal. Easy to type, easy to remember, easy to share.
- **Easy to spell** — Avoid numbers, hyphens, or tricky spellings. If you need to clarify it over the phone, it's too hard.
- **Memorable** — Pick a name with personality. Generic words get forgotten quickly.
- **Relevant** — Hint at what your project does. `blog.sitey.one` beats `stuff.sitey.one`.
